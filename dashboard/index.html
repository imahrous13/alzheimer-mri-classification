1. Cohort & data quality

Original collection is strongly imbalanced (64 moderate / 3,200 non-demented). The augmented collection is larger and more even but not independent clinical evidence.
Validation and test come only from the original images. Augmented copies whose perceptual hash matches a held-out scan are quarantined, never trained on.
Mean Laplacian variance by dataset/class. Augmented images are systematically smoother, a source cue a naive model could exploit.
